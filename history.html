Velocity is wind toward (green) or away from (red) the radar. CC (correlation coefficient) near 1 is plain rain; lower CC inside a strong storm usually means large hail, and a low-CC blob on top of a rotation couplet is the classic tornado debris signature. CC only exists on dual-pol radars. Heights are stretched ×2.5 by default.
