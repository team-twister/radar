Volume render

Volume render draws the whole storm as glowing, see-through fog, like GR2Analyst. It works the graphics chip hard: on older phones (roughly iPhone 11 or earlier, or budget Android phones) it can stutter, drain battery or make the phone warm.

If it stutters, switch Quality to Low in Controls. The app will also lower it by itself if things get too slow.

Velocity is wind toward (green) or away from (red) the radar. CC near 1 (dark red) is plain rain; lower CC inside a strong storm usually means large hail, and a low-CC blob on a rotation couplet is the classic tornado debris signature. CC only exists on dual-pol radars. Heights are stretched ×2.5 by default.
